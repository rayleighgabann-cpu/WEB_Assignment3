About Us

Our Story, Our Team, Our Mission

Our Story

Founded in 2019 by Aigerim Tleubergenova.

Nesibe was born from a dream to bring the flavors of the Kazakh steppe to a modern, welcoming setting. The name means "blessing" in Kazakh.

"I wanted to build a cafe where my grandmother's recipes would not be forgotten."

Aigerim Tleubergenova, Founder

Our Mission

Preserve Tradition

Authentic recipes from different regions of Kazakhstan, sourced from local farms.

Innovate Respectfully

Modern presentations of classic dishes, fusion items that honor both old and new.

Build Community

Cultural events, cooking workshops, and a warm space for families and friends.
